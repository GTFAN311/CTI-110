#Robot Sandwich

##<ins>Assumptions</ins>
1. All of the ingredients for the sandwich are already on the counter in the kitchen out of the packaging. The bread on the left, the turkey meat to the right of that, the cheese slice to the right of that, and the mayonnaise jar to the right of that.
2. The robot is standing at the counter facing the ingredients. Hands empty looking at the ingredients.
3. There is a plate with a butter knife next to the right side of the plate on the counter closer to the robot than the ingredients.
4. The lighting is optimal.
5. The temperature in the house is approxiamately 75 degrees.
6. There is a non-slip mat under the robots feet to ensure solid footing.
7. The robot has non slip fingers so the ingredients don't slip out of it's grip.
8. The robot understands to look where it's hands are moving for the purpose of making this sandwich.
9. The robot knows how much a teaspoon is.

##<ins>Robot Sandwich Instructions</ins>
1. Extend your left arm towards the bread on the counter.
2. Pick up one slice of bread with your finger and thumb.
3. Retract your left arm until your hand is over the plate in front of you.
4. Place the slice of bread in your hand onto the plate under your hand by opening your finger and thumb.
5. Extend your left arm towards the turkey meat on the counter.
6. Pick up one slice of turkey meat with your finger and thumb.
7. Retract your left arm until your hand is over the slice of bread.
8. Place the slice of turkey meat on the bread by opening your finger and thumb.
9. Extend your left arm towards the turkey meat on the counter.
10. Pick up one slice of turkey meat wtih your finger and thumb.
11. Retract your left arm until your hand is over the slice of turkey meat.
12. Place the slice of turkey meat on top of the other slice of turkey meat by opening your finger and thumb.
13. Extend your left hand towards the slice of cheese on the counter.
14. Pick up one slice of cheese with your finger and thumb.
15. Retract your left arm until your hand is over the slice of turkey meat.
16. Place the slice of cheese on the turkey meat with your finger and thumb.
17. Extend your right arm towards the jar of mayonnaise on the counter.
18. Pick up the jar of mayonnaise.
19. Retract your right arm until the jar of mayonnaise is in front of you.
20. Use your right hand to secure the mayonnaise jar.
21. Use your left hand to twist the lid off the mayonnaise jar by turning the lid to the left until it becomes loose.
22. Place the mayonnaise jar lid on the counter.
23. Place the mayonnaise on the counter in front of you again.
24. Extend your left arm to the other slice of bread on the counter.
25. Pick up the slice of bread on the counter.
26. Retract your left arm until the slice of bread is in front of you. 
27. Pick up the butter knife with your right hand.
28. Dip the butter knife into the mayonnaise jar.
29. Scoop one teaspoon of mayonnaise on the butter knife.
30. Spread the mayonnaise on the slice of bread.
31. Place the butter knife on the counter.
32. Extend your left hand until the slice of bread is over the slice of cheese.
33. Turn over the slice of bread until the mayonnaise is face down.
34. Place the slice of bread on the slice of cheese.

##<ins>Done When</ins>
The robot knows the sandwich is complete when it places the last slice of bread with the mayonnaise face down on the slice of cheese.
